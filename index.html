No-spend challenge tracker

A no-spend challenge tracker usually gives you a square to colour each day. Coloured squares do not tell you whether the month is working. Write the categories you are pausing and what you normally spend on them in a week, tick the days you held, log the days you slipped (what, how much), and this page shows your current streak, your longest streak, and the money the challenge has kept in your account so far, per category, after taking off what you did spend.

First visit: the page shows an example challenge started 9 days ago. Press "Clear everything" under the calendar and set up your own.

How it is calculated

Money saved so far, per category = what you usually spend on it in a week, divided by 7, times the days elapsed since the first day (today included), minus the slips you logged in that category. The total is the sum of the categories. It is an estimate built on your own usual spending: if your weekly figures are too high, the saving is too high. It does not count essentials (rent, groceries from the list, fuel), which a no-spend challenge keeps paying.

If you hold to the end = the same calculation over the full length, with the slips logged so far and no new ones.

Current streak = no-spend days in a row, counting back from today (or from yesterday if today is not marked yet). Longest streak = the longest run of no-spend days in the challenge. A past day left blank breaks a streak: mark it, one way or the other.

Rules that make it last: write before day 1 what stays essential, what is paused and what you do instead when the urge comes. Most challenges break on an unplanned purchase in the first week, which is why the slip log asks what you bought: two slips in the same category means that category needs a rule, not more willpower.

Prefer to keep it on paper? The printable no-spend challenge has the rules page (essential, paused, what to do instead), a 30-day tracker, the slip log and an end-of-challenge page to add up the money saved and decide where it goes (US Letter and A4).
